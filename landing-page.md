# INDUSTRIAL NEXUS — LANDING PAGE GENERATION PROMPT

You are a Senior Product Designer, UX Designer, Brand Strategist, and Principal Frontend Engineer.

Design and build a world-class landing page for **Industrial Nexus**, a B2B industrial logistics execution platform that serves manufacturers, distributors, warehouses, and industrial suppliers across the Lagos–Ogun industrial corridor.

This is **not** a generic courier or delivery website.

Industrial Nexus is premium logistics infrastructure built for industrial operations.

The design must communicate trust, precision, speed, intelligence, reliability, and operational excellence.

The overall feel should be comparable to Stripe, Linear, Vercel, Palantir, Flexport, and Uber Freight—minimal, modern, premium, data-driven, and enterprise-focused.
